      What is driving the score

      Every factor below is measured, weighted and shown. Tap one to read why it matters.

        How this score is built

        —

        The score is a weighted index over conditions that are actually measured at or near your spot. Each factor is scored 0–1 from a published fish-behaviour relationship or a long-standing Northern California angling rule, then combined by the weights below. Factors with no data are dropped and the rest reweighted, which is what the confidence figure reports.

        What it does not know

        Whether anyone is catching. It has no creel counts, no hatchery returns, no report from the guy who launched at dawn. A river can read 90 and be empty, or read 40 on the day somebody lands the fish of the season. Treat it as a way to compare hours and days, not as a promise.

        Regulations are not in here

        Seasons, closures, gear rules and limits change every year, and a stale rule shown as fact is worse than none. Run timing below describes when fish are usually present, not when it is legal to fish for them. Check current CDFW regulations →

        Where the numbers come from

        • USGS NWIS — river temperature, flow, stage, turbidity
        • NOAA CO-OPS — tide predictions and station water temperature
        • Open-Meteo — pressure, wind, cloud, rain, sea surface temperature, swell
        • Sun and moon geometry are computed in the page, so solunar and light still work offline.

        Bite Index reads live public data and scores it in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; your spot and units are remembered on this device only.

        Check the weather and the bar before you go. No score is worth a bad crossing.
